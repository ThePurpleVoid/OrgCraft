Agent Team Studio

On a small screen the team is read-only. Edit it on a larger screen.

Switch roles on and off, or drag one onto a manager on the canvas.

Drag a card onto another to change who it reports to. Click a card to edit it.

Who hands work to whom, from each role's "Hands to". Click a role to see its hand-offs: blue goes out, green comes in.

Pick a role on the canvas or in the library.
